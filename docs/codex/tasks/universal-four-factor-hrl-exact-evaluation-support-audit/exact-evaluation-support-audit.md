Take a common positive denominator `scale` and write the exact source point in
primitive integer coordinates:

```text
A=Abar/scale, B=Bbar/scale,
U_i=Ubar_i/scale,
a_i=abar_i/scale, b_i=bbar_i/scale,
```

with

```text
abar_i^2=Ubar_i^2+Abar^2,
bbar_i^2=Ubar_i^2+Bbar^2,
Delta_bar=Abar^2-Bbar^2.
```

Choose the positive source roots and set

```text
q_i=abar_i+bbar_i > 0.
```

Then the exact source identity is

```text
q_i*(abar_i-bbar_i)=Delta_bar,
q_i divides Delta_bar.
```

The four chosen cover radicals are, with independent signs,

```text
r1=2*epsilon1*abar_1/scale,
r2=2*epsilon2*abar_2/scale,
r3=2*eta1*bbar_1/scale,
r4=2*eta2*bbar_2/scale.
```

Put `sigma_i=epsilon_i*eta_i`. Direct evaluation gives

```text
H_RL(P)=4*epsilon1*epsilon2/scale^2 * M(sigma1,sigma2),
```

where

```text
M(+,+) = Delta_bar^2/(q1*q2)
M(+,-) = Delta_bar*q2/q1
M(-,+) = Delta_bar*q1/q2
M(-,-) = q1*q2.
```

Therefore the exact realized squareclass is

```text
[H_RL(P)] = [epsilon1*epsilon2*q1*q2*Delta_bar^e],
e=1 exactly when sigma1 != sigma2.
```

This includes all sixteen choices of the four radical signs. The certificate
checks all four relative-sign cases symbolically and also checks the reduced
source quartic

```text
4*U_i^2*q_i^2
 = (q_i^2-(Abar+Bbar)^2)*(q_i^2-(Abar-Bbar)^2).
```

## Valuation And Boundary Audit

For every prime `p`, the exact parity formula is

```text
v_p(H_RL(P))
 = v_p(q1)+v_p(q2)+e*v_p(Delta_bar)-2*v_p(scale) mod 2.
```

Consequently:

- if `p` does not divide `Delta_bar`, then `q1,q2` are p-units and the realized class is p-even;
- numerator support comes only from `q1,q2` and `Delta_bar`;
- the common denominator contributes an even valuation and no squareclass;
- `g_i=gcd(q_i,Delta_bar/q_i)=gcd(abar_i+bbar_i,abar_i-bbar_i)` divides `2*gcd(abar_i,bbar_i)`; no stronger coprimality is assumed;
- if `Delta_bar` is odd, both `q_i` are odd and the dyadic class is even;
- if `Delta_bar` is even, both `q_i` and `Delta_bar/q_i` are even, and the displayed parity formula is the complete p=2 reduction, with no residue table used.

For the two target branches, the source coordinates are retained separately:

```text
T=A+B or T=abs(A-B).
```

The sum branches have `U_1+U_2=T`, `t=U_1-U_2`, and `|t|<T`. The signed
difference branches have either `U_1-U_2=T` or `U_2-U_1=T`, with
`t=U_1+U_2` and `|t|>T`. Off the midpoint, `x=t^2` is positive and nonzero.

The remaining projective and real boundaries are not dropped:

- `t=0` gives `q1=q2` and is retained as the midpoint;
- `t=+/-T` are the source-coordinate-zero endpoints;
- `r_i=0` is impossible on the positive exact source image, but the factor-zero chart is retained;
- infinity is not an affine source point and is retained as a projective boundary;
- `t=+/-Delta/T` collision fibers are covered by the same source evaluation;
- primitive common denominators cover every finite denominator chart;
- the real sum and signed-difference intervals remain distinct.

## Exact Support Reduction

For each fixed primitive specialization, every realized class lies in the
finite group

```text
G_Delta=<[-1],[p] for primes p dividing Delta_bar>.
```

This is a pointwise support statement proved from exact source coordinates,
not a formal twist statement. It is not yet parameter-uniform: the prime
factorization of `Delta_bar` varies, and the current identities do not prove
that the divisor squareclass `[q1*q2]` belongs to a fixed finite subgroup
generated by a finite list of parameter factors.

The strictly smaller exact proposition now isolated is:

```text
For every primitive exact source tuple on either T branch, determine the
squareclass image of q1*q2 for q_i>0 satisfying

4*U_i^2*q_i^2
 = (q_i^2-(Abar+Bbar)^2)*(q_i^2-(Abar-Bbar)^2),
q_i | Delta_bar,

with U_1+U_2=T or |U_1-U_2|=T on both target branches. Prove that this
image lies in a fixed finite parameter-uniform subgroup, or construct a valid
exact AA/BB family with unbounded support.
```

The missing input is a closure-compatible divisor-allocation or squareclass
theorem for `q1*q2`, including shared gcds at primes dividing `Delta_bar` and
the even branch at `p=2`. No such theorem follows from the source equations
alone.
