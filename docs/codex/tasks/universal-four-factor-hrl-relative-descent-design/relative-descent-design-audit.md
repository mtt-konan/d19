## Twist Quotient

Before common-scale quotienting, work separately over

```text
K=Q(A,B), T=A+B,
K=Q(A,B), T=A-B with A>B>0.
```

The intrinsic constant twist equivalence for
`Y^2=d*H_RL` is exactly

```text
d ~ d'  iff  d/d' is in K^{*2},
```

so the raw parameter quotient is

```text
K^*/K^{*2}.
```

This is exact because the generic four-factor cover is geometrically integral
and has constant field `K`; a constant square in `K(C)` is already a square in
`K`.

After the common-scale chart `lambda=A/B`, the scale-quotiented parameter
field is `k=Q(lambda)`. The two retained charts are

```text
T/B=lambda+1,
T/B=lambda-1 with lambda>1.
```

Their scale-quotiented intrinsic twist quotient is

```text
k^*/k^{*2}.
```

The `t -> -t`, plus-chart `A <-> B`, and physical-minus signed-chart
reordering preserve the `H_RL` class and add no further twist factor.

At a fixed rational specialization this becomes `Q^*/Q^{*2}`. Arbitrary
rational prime classes remain present until an arithmetic local or global
condition removes them.

If radical sign choices are identified by deck-equivalent presentations, the
known finite orbit subgroup is generated by `[-1]` and `[Delta]`. Hence the
optional presentation quotient is

```text
K^*/(K^{*2}*<-1,[Delta]>)

or, after scale,

k^*/(k^{*2}*<-1,lambda^2-1>).
```

This orbit quotient is not an intrinsic equality of twists on one fixed
oriented cover; it only identifies twists related by the deck sign action.

## Support Audit

The finite geometric boundary candidate is supported by factors of

```text
2*A*B*T*Delta*(T^2+4*A^2)*(T^2+4*B^2)
  *(T^2+(A-B)^2)*(T^2+(A+B)^2).
```

After scale, its plus and physical-minus specializations are recorded
separately by the executable certificate. The twist quotient is not supported
on this finite set at the generic design level. For every rational
`c != +/-1`, take

```text
d_c=lambda-c.
```

At the parameter divisor `(lambda-c)`,

```text
v_(lambda-c)(d_c)=1,
v_(lambda-c)(lambda^2-1)=0.
```

Distinct `c` therefore give distinct squareclasses in the raw quotient and
remain distinct after quotienting by `[-1]` and `[lambda^2-1]`. This proves
that neither exact twist quotient has finite parameter-uniform support
inherited from the geometric boundary data.

This is a support obstruction at the design layer, not a claim that infinitely
many of these twists contain points satisfying the exact AA/BB conditions.
The latter is precisely the missing arithmetic input.

All required restrictions remain explicit in the certificate: both `T`
branches, positive square `x=t^2`, sum and signed-difference real intervals,
all four factor lifts, exact AA/BB image, `p=2`, primitive/gcd and denominator
conditions, midpoint, endpoints, factor-zero, infinity, collision, and
denominator charts. No local-prime table was run.

## Outcome

```text
accepted_outcome=precise_twist_support_blocker
```

The missing theorem/data is a parameter-uniform global divisor, norm, or exact
AA/BB-lift relation proving that every twist class realized on the restricted
image lies in a finite support subgroup. The existing four-factor lifts,
dyadic/denominator retention, and real or boundary conditions do not prove
such a relation. Until that input exists, a finite twist-support group cannot
be declared and no local-prime table is authorized.
