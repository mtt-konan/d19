# Next Research Gate

This page routes task selection. It does not duplicate the mathematical status
in [PROJECT_STATUS.md](PROJECT_STATUS.md) or turn an old checkpoint into an
automatic research assignment.

## Task Selection And Recovery

The current user request selects the work. The
[current context](codex/current-context.md) holds the latest recorded research
entry; follow its task anchor for detailed status, scope, and next step. Use
the [task index](codex/index.md) to locate other branches when needed.

This page owns selection rules, not a second copy of task status. A recovery
pointer does not resume research during an unrelated request. Other branches
retain their own anchors and permissions.

## Selecting A Narrow Task

When the user resumes research, record the chosen task in its own anchor:

1. State the exact proposition, quantifiers, domain, and required conclusion.
2. Identify the information and constraints actually used, and the new input
   relative to any relevant prior attempt.
3. State what would count as progress or refute the proposed idea.
4. Retain all original conditions needed for the claim, including complete
   lifts and exceptional branches if a reduction is used.
5. Bound any computation by its proof or falsification purpose. A larger empty
   search alone cannot establish the global statement.

The [research preference](OPEN_DIRECTIONS.md) guides candidate evaluation;
it does not select a descent, Selmer calculation, or HRL continuation. A
projected witness becomes an original counterexample only after every
required lift and reconstruction condition is satisfied.

## Using Earlier Work

Consult the [route archive](archive/RESEARCH_ROUTES.md) only for the candidate
under consideration. Its outcome and reopening conditions remain evidence;
its old imperative handoffs are not current instructions. In particular, a
scoped route-death does not forbid every method with the same broad label.

Archiving does not authorize shell 11, new support families, broad diagnostic
runs, or D2/D5/M2/M3. A separate scoped selection is required. An independent
proof need not pass through the old Selmer or divisor-pair route.
