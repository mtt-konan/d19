# Research route archive

Archived from the default handoff on 2026-09-12. This is a reference index,
not an execution queue. Mathematical artifacts remain at their original
paths; no proof, script, test, or other task anchor is moved or rewritten.

[PROJECT_STATUS.md](../PROJECT_STATUS.md) owns the mathematical status.
[NEXT_RESEARCH_GATE.md](../NEXT_RESEARCH_GATE.md) owns new task selection.
Historical "next gate", "must", and "do not create" language is scoped to
its original task. It does not automatically select work in a new session.
Proved facts, exact counterexamples to proposed mechanisms, and scope limits
remain evidence and must not be erased by this navigation change.

## Status meanings

- Completed scoped result: the named proof or audit is finished, not the
  original global problem.
- Paused: a precise unresolved obligation remains; the route is not disproved.
- Scoped route-death: the specified mechanism cannot deliver its proposed
  conclusion under the audited inputs. This is not a ban on the whole field.
- Finite diagnostic: bounded evidence, without a uniform theorem bridge.
- Independent task: retains its own status and scope; not selected here.
