# AA+BB D3 Torsion Baseline in d-Coordinates

## Question

After `wl471` defines the complete `d`-coordinate container, `wl472` confines
the relevant support, and `wl473` frames the unsolved local `C_d` row
obligations, how should the rational torsion baseline be carried into the
future matrix step?

## Result

`D3_torsion_baseline_in_d_coordinates` records the rational `E_L[2](Q)` Kummer
baseline directly in the same complete `d`-coordinate quotient as D0-D2.

The ambient quotient remains

```text
V = Q*/Q*2,
```

with coordinate order

```text
d_minus_p       = X-(-p)=X+p
d_pos_2sqrt_q   = X-(2*sqrt_q)
d_neg_2sqrt_q   = X-(-2*sqrt_q)=X+2*sqrt_q
```

and relation

```text
d_minus_p*d_pos_2sqrt_q*d_neg_2sqrt_q = 1 in V.
```

The baseline representatives are:

```text
O:
  (1, 1, 1)

T_minus_p=(-p,0):
  ((-p-2*sqrt_q)*(-p+2*sqrt_q),
   -p-2*sqrt_q,
   -p+2*sqrt_q)

T_pos_2sqrt_q=(2*sqrt_q,0):
  (2*sqrt_q+p,
   (2*sqrt_q+p)*(4*sqrt_q),
   4*sqrt_q)

T_neg_2sqrt_q=(-2*sqrt_q,0):
  (-2*sqrt_q+p,
   -4*sqrt_q,
   (-2*sqrt_q+p)*(-4*sqrt_q))
```

At a root `X=e_i`, the complete-descent representative uses the standard rule:
replace the vanishing coordinate by the product of the two nonzero root
differences. Thus the three nonzero rational torsion representatives are
recorded as complete `d`-data, not through the old package `x`-coordinate
collapse.

## Product Relation Checks

Each representative lies on the D0 product-relation plane:

```text
O:
  1*1*1 = 1

T_minus_p:
  ((-p-2*sqrt_q)*(-p+2*sqrt_q))*(-p-2*sqrt_q)*(-p+2*sqrt_q)
    = ((-p-2*sqrt_q)*(-p+2*sqrt_q))^2

T_pos_2sqrt_q:
  (2*sqrt_q+p)*((2*sqrt_q+p)*(4*sqrt_q))*(4*sqrt_q)
    = ((2*sqrt_q+p)*(4*sqrt_q))^2

T_neg_2sqrt_q:
  (-2*sqrt_q+p)*(-4*sqrt_q)*((-2*sqrt_q+p)*(-4*sqrt_q))
    = ((-2*sqrt_q+p)*(-4*sqrt_q))^2
```

So all four products are `1` in `V`.

## Generator Choice

The baseline has dimension `2`. In D3 it is generated by

```text
T_minus_p, T_pos_2sqrt_q.
```

The coordinatewise product relations in `V^3` are:

```text
T_minus_p*T_minus_p = O
T_pos_2sqrt_q*T_pos_2sqrt_q = O
T_neg_2sqrt_q*T_neg_2sqrt_q = O

T_minus_p*T_pos_2sqrt_q = T_neg_2sqrt_q
T_minus_p*T_neg_2sqrt_q = T_pos_2sqrt_q
T_pos_2sqrt_q*T_neg_2sqrt_q = T_minus_p
```

The baseline is recorded, not quotiented out.

## Later Comparison Contract

When the D2 local `C_d` rows are actually solved in a later step, each of

```text
O, T_minus_p, T_pos_2sqrt_q, T_neg_2sqrt_q
```

must be compared against every solved local row in the same complete
`d`-coordinate order. Only after that comparison may a later D4-style rank
ledger or quotient computation refer to the torsion baseline.

D3 does not solve the D2 rows and does not build the D4 matrix. The next
mathematical work is to solve the D2 local `C_d` rows, not to jump directly to
D4.
